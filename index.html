The problem in our backyard

Plastic is washing into the waters we love

25,082 lbs · 71 sites · 3,615 volunteers

Miami-Dade’s 2024 International Coastal Cleanup collected 25,082 pounds of trash at 71 sites with 3,615 volunteers — described by organizers as the 3rd-largest county ICC program in the U.S. after LA and San Francisco. Source: VolunteerCleanup.org.

FIU researchers reported an average microplastic abundance of 7.16 ± 1.57 particles/m³ in Biscayne Bay, mainly polypropylene (40%) and polyethylene (24%) — the same polyolefin families used in recycled-plastic lumber. Source: Marine Pollution Bulletin (FIU, 2026).

Photos: Cap-Haïtien, Haiti (Caribbean shoreline plastic — not Miami) · Florida Keys National Marine Sanctuary cleanup (NOAA). Credits
