Why plastic lumber

High-performance boards and furniture made from recycled plastic — built for Florida sun, salt air, and heavy public use.

🌊

Never rot

Does not rot, splinter, or absorb moisture. Ideal for waterfront decks, boardwalks, and outdoor seating.

🛠️

No paint or sealing

UV-protected pigments and weather resistance mean lower lifetime maintenance than traditional wood.

♻️

Recycled origin

Made from recycled plastics, supporting a circular economy and giving waste a second life as public good.
