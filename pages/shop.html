SHOPIFY BUY BUTTON — CONFIGURE HERE
1. Create products in your Shopify admin and copy each product ID into the matching data-shopify-product-id on the cards above (pendant, planter, bench, coasters, laptop stand, phone stand, beach paddle).
2. Uncomment the script block at the bottom of this page.
3. Replace SHOPIFY_DOMAIN (e.g. your-store.myshopify.com) and STOREFRONT_ACCESS_TOKEN.
4. Set each buy-slot’s data-configured="true" once its button renders (Inquire stays as fallback until then).
