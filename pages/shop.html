DIY project ideas

Planter box

A 3 ft × 1 ft box, two boards high: about 3 × 1x4 for the walls and floor plus 1 × 1x2 for corner posts. [confirm]

Garden bench

A 4 ft backless bench: about 4 × 1x4 for the seat, aprons, and doubled legs plus 1 × 1x2 for bracing. [confirm]

Raised bed

A 4 ft × 2 ft bed, three boards high (about 12 in): about 5 × 1x4 for the walls plus 1 × 1x2 for corner stakes. [confirm]

Tips: cut with a carbide-tipped blade at moderate speed, and use exterior-grade (stainless or coated) wood screws. A pilot hole makes driving screws easier. Leave a small gap where long boards meet so they can move with the heat.
