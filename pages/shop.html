DIY project ideas

Planter box

A long, low box for herbs or flowers: 1x4 walls and floor, with 1x2 corner posts.

Garden bench

A simple backless bench: 1x4 for the seat, aprons, and doubled legs, with 1x2 for bracing.

Raised bed

A rot-proof garden bed: stacked 1x4 walls with 1x2 corner stakes. Tell us your size and we’ll help work out how many boards you need.

Tips: cut with a carbide-tipped blade at moderate speed, and use exterior-grade (stainless or coated) wood screws. A pilot hole makes driving screws easier. Leave a small gap where long boards meet so they can move with the heat.
