Bay-focused positioning

Everwood USA’s story is local: plastic that threatens coastal ecosystems can become furniture along the same shoreline.

Market focus — Biscayne Bay, Miami, and Miami Beach — ties material circularity to places residents and visitors already love.

  • Reduces demand for maintenance-heavy outdoor wood in humid, salty climates
  • Gives brands a physical CSR footprint near the Bay
  • Supports municipal and campus sustainability narratives with durable assets

Traceability & circular story

Furniture can carry a QR plaque so visitors can learn the product story — where the plastic came from and why it never rots. Per everwood-usa.com, products are also positioned to support a circular economy and the SDGs.

Desafío Ambiente emphasizes eco-design so products can be recyclable again at end of life — closing the loop rather than creating a one-way “downcycle.”
