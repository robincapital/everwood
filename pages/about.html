Who we are

Locally owned in Miami Beach

Everwood USA is a locally owned Miami Beach company, in partnership with Desafío Ambiente / Everwood Chile — turning local plastic into lumber and public goods that never rot.

Founded in Miami Beach

Everwood USA is owned and operated by founder Nick Diaz, a Miami Beach resident and longtime divemaster who is regularly on the water cleaning up the Bay. He has funded and built the company for years with a local team. The mission is to preserve our waterways and beaches and build a circular economy where local plastic becomes local public goods.

A Miami Beach business owner, Nick sees Everwood USA as giving back to the community — not as a sales pitch, but as a way to keep plastic out of the water and put lasting furniture and materials into local parks, campuses, and shorelines.

Our partner: Desafío Ambiente

Manufacturing and innovation partner in Chile — not a parent company. Everwood USA is independent and locally owned.

Desafío Ambiente / Everwood Chile designs and manufactures the recycled-plastic lumber and products. Founder Anita Farfán leads the Everwood brand under Desafío Ambiente (desafioambiente.com).

Reported group-wide figures from 2023–24 press (Chile, USA, and Peru — not US-only): about 3,000 tonnes of plastic recovered over ~8 years; 140+ company partners; 140+ projects; reported 3,300 t CO₂ reduced and 1,500 trees not felled. Sources listed on our Impact page and in project research notes.

The partnership brings their proven manufacturing and innovation to Miami, with capacity to expand here as demand grows.
