Who we are

From Chilean innovation to Miami’s Bay

Everwood USA is the US branch of Desafío Ambiente / Everwood — turning garbage and recycled plastic into wood-like boards and lasting public furniture.

Our story

Everwood began as a project of Desafío Ambiente in Santiago, Chile, where recycled plastics are revalorized through injection and extrusion into high-performance construction materials and urban furniture.

Everwood USA brings that model to South Florida — with a focus on Biscayne Bay, Miami, and Miami Beach — so local plastic waste can become benches, decks, fencing, and site furniture that never rot.

Founder Anita Farfán leads the Everwood brand under Desafío Ambiente (source: desafioambiente.com).

How Desafío Ambiente works

Adapted from desafioambiente.com — the parent company’s three pillars.

♻️

Revalorize

Every shredded plastic particle enters injection or extrusion lines to become durable, quality products.

📐

Eco-design

Before transformation, materials are analyzed and eco-designed so products remain recyclable at end of life.

💡

Advise

Strategic guidance for companies on eco-design and circular economy decisions around their waste streams.

Positioning
