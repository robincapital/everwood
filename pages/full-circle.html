Community program

Full Circle

Join Miami Beach and Biscayne Bay cleanups. The plastic you collect is fabricated into Everwood products — pendants and furniture — and you can buy back a piece made from the trash you picked up. Every piece carries a QR tag linking to its cleanup.

Plastic you pick up can come back as something lasting

Full Circle connects local cleanups to Everwood fabrication. Collected plastic is sorted and recycled into materials and finished goods. A QR plaque or tag on each piece points back to the cleanup date, beach, and pounds collected.

See the Shop

How it works

Four steps from shoreline to finished piece.

1

Clean up

Join a scheduled Miami Beach or Biscayne Bay cleanup. Founded and run locally by Miami Beach resident, freediver, and divemaster Nick Diaz, often joined by Nick’s kids, and our local team. Bring gloves and friends — we’ll share meeting details when you sign up.

2

We sort & recycle

Collected plastic is sorted and prepared for recycling into Everwood feedstock.

3

We fabricate

Material becomes products — from Bay Edition pendants to furniture — with a QR tag for that cleanup’s story.

4

Wear it / sit on it

Buy back a piece tied to the plastic from cleanups you joined. Scan the QR to see date, beach, and pounds collected.

Traceability on every piece

QR tags link each product to its cleanup — the same idea as the plaques on our furniture.
