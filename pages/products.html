Built for green building

Everwood products can contribute toward LEED v4.1 Materials & Resources credits — specifically Sourcing of Raw Materials (recycled content). LEED certifies buildings, not products. Ask us about available documentation.

*Not a USGBC or LEED product certification. Claiming recycled-content contribution on a project typically requires an ISO 14021 recycled-content declaration from the factory (post- vs pre-consumer % by weight). Ask us about the current status of that declaration before you rely on it for a project. Listed in IDIEM’s Catálogo Verde (Chile) — a product listing, not a test certification. No ISO management-system, B Corp, or fire-rating claims are made here.
