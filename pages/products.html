Materials & furniture

Plastic lumber that works like wood — and lasts longer outdoors

Boards, beams, posts, decking, and urban furniture made from recycled plastic. Adapted for a US audience from Everwood / Desafío Ambiente product lines.

Construction materials

Product families from everwood.cl / Desafío Ambiente — English descriptions for US buyers. Dimensions and colors below are from the Chilean catalog; US availability and sizing [confirm US SKUs].

Boards (Tablas)

Ecological boards for cladding, privacy screens, benches, and walls. Smooth surface, UV-protective pigments, workable with standard carpentry tools (cut, drill, sand).

Example catalog size (Chile): 10 × 2.5 × 280 cm. 15-year design warranty stated on everwood.cl.

Deck boards

Decking boards for outdoor floors and waterfront surfaces — moisture-resistant and low maintenance compared with traditional wood decks.

Beams (Vigas)

Robust structural elements for frames, benches, walls, pergolas, and decks. Soft to the touch; UV-protected; carpentry-friendly.

Example catalog size (Chile): 40 × 90 × 2800 mm.

Posts & poles

Round posts (polines), square posts, and heavy cuartón sections for fences, sleepers, pergolas, and foundations. Do not absorb liquids; do not rot or splinter.

Strips, logs & connectors

Listones (strips), rollizos, and deck connectors complete the system for fencing, furniture frames, and deck installs (everwood.cl materiales catalog).

Urban furniture

Benches, picnic tables, lounge chairs, and recycling points designed for universities, parks, companies, and municipalities (desafioambiente.com mobiliario).
